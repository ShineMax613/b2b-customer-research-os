# B2B Customer Research OS

An open-source customer research and lead analysis system for B2B exporters, manufacturers, and global sales teams.

This project helps export sales teams analyze potential customers before sending quotations, catalogs, price lists, or follow-up messages.

## Who This Project Is For

This project is designed for:

* B2B exporters
* Manufacturers
* International sales teams
* Foreign trade salespeople
* LinkedIn prospecting teams
* WhatsApp sales teams
* Small and medium export businesses
* Companies selling to overseas distributors, wholesalers, contractors, hotels, retailers, and project buyers

## Problems It Solves

Many B2B export teams receive leads from Facebook, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, exhibitions, websites, referrals, and cold outreach.

But before sending a quotation, they often need to answer:

* Is this customer real?
* Is this person a decision maker?
* Is this buyer an end user, distributor, trader, contractor, or competitor?
* Does this lead have real buying intent?
* Should we send a full price list?
* Should we ask for more information first?
* How should we follow up professionally?
* Is this lead worth spending time on?

This project provides practical templates, checklists, workflows, and AI prompts to support better customer research and smarter follow-up decisions.

## Core Modules

### 1. Company Background Check

Analyze the company, business type, website, location, social media presence, market position, and possible buying needs.

### 2. Person Role Analysis

Understand whether the contact is likely to be an owner, procurement manager, operations manager, project manager, reseller, competitor, or low-quality lead.

### 3. Buyer Intent Analysis

Evaluate the customer's message, product requirement, quantity, delivery city, urgency, company information, and quotation readiness.

### 4. End User vs Distributor vs Competitor Analysis

Identify the likely role of the customer in the supply chain before deciding what information to share.

### 5. Red Flag Checklist

Avoid wasting time on fake buyers, price hunters, unclear inquiries, and competitors trying to collect price information.

### 6. Follow-up Strategy

Choose the right next message based on lead quality, buyer intent, risk level, and available information.

### 7. AI Prompts

Use AI to assist with customer research, lead analysis, message writing, and follow-up strategy.

## Suggested Workflow

1. Collect customer information
2. Check company background
3. Analyze the contact person's role
4. Evaluate buyer intent
5. Identify whether the buyer is an end user, distributor, trader, contractor, or competitor
6. Score lead quality
7. Check possible red flags
8. Decide whether to send a catalog, quotation, price list, or ask more questions first
9. Write the next follow-up message
10. Update the lead status
