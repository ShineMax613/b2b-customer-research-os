# Price Hunter Red Flags Checklist

Use this checklist to identify possible price hunters before sending a quotation, catalog, full price list, or sensitive product information.

This checklist is designed for B2B exporters, manufacturers, wholesalers, and international sales teams.

## 2. Important Reminder

A customer asking for price first is not always a bad lead.

In many B2B markets, buyers may start with:

- Price?
- Send price list
- Best price?
- Catalog and price?
- Wholesale price?

This does not automatically mean the customer is fake or low quality.

The goal of this checklist is not to reject price-focused buyers too quickly.

The goal is to decide how much information to share before the buyer is properly qualified.

## 3. Common Price Hunter Signals

Check all that apply:

- Asks for price immediately without product details
- Asks for full price list before introducing company
- Asks for many unrelated product prices
- Does not mention quantity
- Does not mention delivery city
- Does not share company name
- Does not explain buying purpose
- Refuses to answer basic qualification questions
- Keeps asking for “last price” or “best price” only
- Compares price without discussing quality, stock, delivery, or service
- Requests sensitive pricing for many SKUs
- Asks for supplier, factory, or source information too early
- Communication becomes silent after receiving price
- Uses vague identity or personal profile only
- Profile does not clearly match the claimed business

## 4. Stronger Risk Signals

These signals may suggest higher risk:

- The customer asks for a complete price list immediately
- The customer asks for prices across many unrelated categories
- The customer refuses to provide quantity
- The customer refuses to provide delivery location
- The customer asks detailed supplier or factory questions without clear purchase plan
- The customer wants quotation documents but gives no company details
- The customer pushes for price but avoids business verification
- The customer asks for your full catalog and price list but does not state any demand
- The customer appears to be from a competing business
- The customer’s profile shows selling similar products

## 5. Signals That Reduce Risk

The lead may still be valuable if they provide:

- Company name
- Business type
- Specific product requirement
- Quantity
- Delivery city
- Project name or usage scenario
- Buying timeline
- Regular purchasing plan
- Clear resale or distribution purpose
- Relevant job title or buyer role
- Professional communication
- Willingness to answer follow-up questions

## 6. Price Hunter Risk Level

Choose one:

### Low Risk

The customer asks about price, but also provides enough useful information.

Examples:

- Company name is clear
- Product requirement is specific
- Quantity is mentioned
- Delivery city is mentioned
- Buying purpose is clear

Recommended action:

- Send item-based quotation
- Share selected catalog pages
- Continue normal follow-up

### Medium Risk

The customer asks mainly for price, but the inquiry is still relevant.

Examples:

- Product category is clear
- Quantity is missing
- Delivery city is missing
- Company background is unclear
- Customer may still be a real buyer

Recommended action:

- Ask qualification questions first
- Send selected product photos or catalog pages
- Avoid sending full price list
- Quote only after basic details are confirmed

### High Risk

The customer appears to collect price information without clear buying intent.

Examples:

- No company name
- No quantity
- No delivery city
- No clear product requirement
- Asks for full price list immediately
- Avoids answering basic questions
- May be a competitor or trader collecting prices

Recommended action:

- Do not send full price list
- Do not share sensitive commercial terms
- Ask for company details, quantity, and delivery city
- Share only limited general product information
- Stop following if they remain vague

## 7. Qualification Questions to Ask

Use these questions before sending detailed pricing:

1. May I know your company name?
2. Which items are you interested in?
3. How many cartons or pieces do you need?
4. Which city or country should we deliver to?
5. Are you buying for your own hotel, project, store, or for resale?
6. Do you need regular stock items or customized branding?
7. Is this for immediate purchase, project planning, or price comparison?

## 8. Suggested WhatsApp Reply

Hi [Name], thank you for your inquiry.

To prepare an accurate quotation, may I know which items you need, the quantity, and the delivery city?

We can then check availability and send you the most suitable offer based on your actual requirement.

## 9. Suggested Direct Reply for Full Price List Requests

Hi [Name], thank you for your interest.

Because our prices depend on item, quantity, packaging, stock availability, and delivery location, we usually prepare quotations based on confirmed requirements instead of sending a general full price list.

Please share the items you need, quantity, and delivery city, and we will prepare a suitable offer for you.
