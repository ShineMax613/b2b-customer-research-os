# Example: Hotel Supplies Facebook Lead Analysis

This example shows how to analyze a Facebook ad lead for hotel supplies before sending a quotation or full price list.

All information in this example is anonymized. It does not include real customer names, phone numbers, private messages, or confidential quotation details.

## 1. Lead Source

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| Lead source | Facebook ad lead |
| Industry | Hotel supplies |
| Market | Saudi Arabia / GCC |
| Product category | Hotel guest amenities |
| Contact channel | Facebook Messenger / WhatsApp |
| Buyer type | Unknown at first contact |

## 2. Original Inquiry Summary

The lead asked about hotel toiletries and guest amenities.

The requested items may include:

- Hotel shampoo
- Shower gel
- Soap
- Body lotion
- Guestroom amenities
- Disposable hotel supplies

The first inquiry was short and price-focused.

## 3. Available Information

Information available from the first message:

- The customer is interested in hotel supplies
- The customer may need guest amenities
- The customer is likely located in Saudi Arabia or GCC
- The customer may be a hotel buyer, distributor, furnished apartment operator, or reseller

Information not yet confirmed:

- Company name
- Buyer role
- Whether they are buying for own use or resale
- Exact items required
- Quantity
- Delivery city
- Urgency
- Custom branding requirement

## 4. Buyer Intent Analysis

### Positive Signals

- The inquiry is related to a clear product category
- The product category matches the seller's business
- Guest amenities are repeat-purchase products
- The lead came through a targeted Facebook ad
- The customer may have an active procurement need

### Weak Signals

- The customer did not provide quantity
- The customer did not provide company information
- The customer did not specify delivery city
- The customer may be comparing prices
- The customer may ask for a full price list before qualification

## 5. Possible Buyer Type

The lead may be one of the following:

### Possible End User

The lead may be an end user if they are buying for:

- Hotel
- Furnished apartment
- Resort
- Motel
- Serviced apartment
- Guest accommodation project

### Possible Distributor / Wholesaler

The lead may be a distributor if they:

- Ask for wholesale price
- Ask for full catalog
- Ask for many SKUs
- Ask about carton quantity
- Ask about fast-moving items
- Mention resale

### Possible Price Hunter / Competitor

The lead may be a price hunter or competitor if they:

- Ask only for price
- Refuse to share company information
- Ask for full price list immediately
- Ask many product prices without quantity
- Avoid answering basic qualification questions

## 6. Lead Quality Score

Estimated initial score:

| Criteria | Score |
|---|---|
| Company information provided | 0 / 15 |
| Clear product requirement | 15 / 20 |
| Quantity mentioned | 0 / 20 |
| Delivery city mentioned | 0 / 10 |
| Contact role is relevant | 5 / 15 |
| Urgency or timeline mentioned | 0 / 10 |
| Communication quality | 5 / 10 |
| Total | 25 / 100 |

Initial classification:

**Low-priority lead / Need more information**

This does not mean the lead is bad. It means the lead needs qualification before quotation.

## 7. Risk Signals

Possible risks:

- Price-only inquiry
- No quantity
- No company information
- No delivery city
- No buyer role
- Possible full price list request
- Possible distributor or competitor price checking

## 8. Recommended Next Step

Do not send a full price list immediately.

Recommended action:

1. Thank the customer
2. Ask for exact items
3. Ask for quantity by carton or pieces
4. Ask for delivery city
5. Ask whether they buy for own use or resale
6. Offer to check availability and prepare a suitable quotation

## 9. Suggested WhatsApp Reply

Hi [Name], thank you for your inquiry.

We can supply hotel guest amenities such as shampoo, shower gel, soap, body lotion, and other hotel room items.

To prepare a suitable quotation, may I know:

1. Which items do you need?
2. How many cartons / pieces do you need?
3. Which city should we deliver to?
4. Are you buying for your own hotel / apartment / project, or for resale?

Once we have these details, we can check availability and prepare the most suitable offer for you.

## 10. Internal Sales Notes

Recommended internal action:

- Do not send full price list at first contact
- Send selected product photos or catalog pages only if needed
- Quote based on confirmed item and quantity
- If the buyer is a distributor, ask about regular demand and fast-moving items
- If the buyer is an end user, focus on stock, delivery, consistency, and product quality
- If the buyer refuses to provide basic details, keep communication limited

## 11. Final Decision

Recommended decision:

**Ask more questions first.**

Reason:

The lead shows possible interest in hotel supplies, but the buying intent is not clear enough for a full quotation. The salesperson should qualify the customer before sharing detailed pricing.
