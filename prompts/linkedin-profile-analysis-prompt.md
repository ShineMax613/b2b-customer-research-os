# LinkedIn Profile Analysis Prompt

Use this prompt to analyze a LinkedIn prospect profile before sending a connection request, direct message, catalog, quotation, or follow-up message.

This prompt is designed for B2B exporters, manufacturers, international sales teams, LinkedIn prospecting teams, and overseas business development operators.

## Prompt

Act as a B2B export sales research analyst.

Your task is to analyze a LinkedIn prospect profile based on the information I provide.

Please help me determine:

1. Whether this person is likely relevant to my target customer profile
2. What role this person may have in the buying process
3. Whether they are likely to be an end user, distributor, wholesaler, contractor, consultant, trader, or competitor
4. Whether they may have decision-making power or influence
5. What business opportunities may exist
6. What risks or red flags I should notice
7. Whether I should connect, follow, message, nurture, or avoid
8. What first LinkedIn message I should send

## Analysis Requirements

Please analyze the prospect using the following structure:

### 1. Profile Summary

Summarize who this person appears to be based on the provided LinkedIn information.

### 2. Relevance to Target Customer Profile

Classify relevance as:

- High relevance
- Medium relevance
- Low relevance
- Unknown

Explain your reasoning.

Consider:

- Industry match
- Job title
- Company type
- Market location
- Business activity
- Possible purchasing responsibility
- Possible influence over supplier decisions

### 3. Possible Buyer Type

Classify the prospect as one or more of the following:

- End user
- Distributor
- Wholesaler
- Retailer
- Contractor
- Consultant
- Project buyer
- Hotel / resort / furnished apartment operator
- Procurement professional
- Operations professional
- Competitor
- Service provider
- Unknown

Explain your reasoning.

### 4. Role in Buying Process

Analyze whether the person may be:

- Final decision maker
- Procurement decision maker
- Technical evaluator
- Operations influencer
- Project coordinator
- Reseller buyer
- Distributor buyer
- Gatekeeper
- Competitor researcher
- Low-priority contact
- Unknown

Estimate decision power:

- High
- Medium
- Low
- Unknown

Explain your reasoning.

### 5. Opportunity Analysis

Identify possible business opportunities.

Consider:

- Product fit
- Market fit
- Company size or activity
- Possible repeat purchasing needs
- Possible project needs
- Possible distribution or wholesale opportunity
- Possible relationship-building value

### 6. Risk Signals

List any possible risk signals, such as:

- Profile has little business activity
- Job title is unclear
- Company does not match target market
- Person appears to be a competitor
- Person appears to be a service seller only
- No clear connection to purchasing or operations
- Profile looks inactive
- Profile information is too limited
- Connection may not be worth immediate outreach

Important: Do not assume someone is a competitor without evidence.

### 7. Recommended LinkedIn Action

Choose one:

- Send connection request
- Follow first, do not message yet
- Send soft introduction message
- Send value-based message
- Nurture with content
- Do not contact now
- Need more research first

Explain why.

### 8. Suggested Connection Request Message

Write a short LinkedIn connection request message.

The message should be:

- Short
- Professional
- Natural
- Not too salesy
- Relevant to the person’s role or industry
- Suitable for B2B export relationship building

### 9. Suggested First Message After Connection

Write a first message after the person accepts the connection.

The message should:

- Thank them for connecting
- Avoid aggressive selling
- Build trust
- Mention relevant industry context
- Invite light conversation
- Avoid sending catalog or price list immediately

### 10. Suggested Follow-up Strategy

Recommend how to follow up over time.

Include:

- What content to share
- When to ask about needs
- When to introduce products
- When to move to WhatsApp or email
- When to stop following

### 12. Final Recommendation

Choose one:

- High-priority prospect
- Qualified prospect
- Nurture prospect
- Low-priority prospect
- Not suitable
- Need more information

Explain why.

## Important Rules

Do not make unsupported claims.

If information is missing, say “unknown” instead of guessing.

Do not assume someone has purchasing power only because of a senior title.

Do not use spammy or aggressive outreach.

Do not recommend sending catalog or price list in the first message unless there is clear buying intent.

Focus on practical B2B sales decisions.
