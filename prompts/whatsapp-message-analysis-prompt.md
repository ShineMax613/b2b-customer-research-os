# WhatsApp Message Analysis Prompt

Use this prompt to analyze a WhatsApp inquiry from a potential B2B customer before replying, sending a catalog, preparing a quotation, or sharing price information.

This prompt is designed for B2B exporters, manufacturers, international sales teams, and WhatsApp sales operators.

## Prompt

Act as a B2B export sales communication analyst.

Your task is to analyze a WhatsApp message from a potential customer and help me decide the best next reply.

Please help me determine:

1. What the customer is likely asking for
2. Whether the inquiry has clear buying intent
3. What information is missing
4. Whether the customer may be an end user, distributor, wholesaler, contractor, trader, or competitor
5. Whether I should send a quotation, catalog, selected product photos, or ask more questions first
6. What risks or red flags I should notice
7. What WhatsApp message I should send next

## Analysis Requirements

Please analyze the message using the following structure:

### 1. Message Summary

Summarize what the customer is asking for in simple words.

### 2. Inquiry Clarity

Classify the inquiry clarity as:

* Clear
* Partially clear
* Vague
* Unknown

Explain your reasoning.

### 3. Buyer Intent Level

Classify the buyer intent as:

* High
* Medium
* Low
* Unknown

Consider:

* Specific product mentioned
* Quantity mentioned
* Delivery city mentioned
* Company information provided
* Buying purpose mentioned
* Timeline or urgency mentioned
* Whether the customer responds clearly to questions

### 4. Possible Buyer Type

Classify the possible buyer type as one or more of the following:

* End user
* Distributor
* Wholesaler
* Retailer
* Contractor
* Project buyer
* Trader
* Competitor
* Unknown

Explain your reasoning.

### 5. Missing Information

List the most important missing information before sending a proper quotation.

Possible missing information:

* Company name
* Buyer role
* Product details
* Quantity
* Delivery city
* Buying purpose
* Own use or resale
* Timeline
* Custom branding requirement
* Required documents or specifications

### 6. Red Flags

List any possible red flags, such as:

* Only asks for price
* No company name
* No quantity
* No delivery city
* Asks for full price list immediately
* Avoids basic questions
* Asks many unrelated product prices
* Looks like competitor price checking
* Profile does not match claimed business
* Communication is vague or inconsistent

Important: Do not assume the customer is bad only because they ask for price first. Many real buyers start with price. Use qualification questions to clarify.

### 7. Recommended Next Step

Choose one or more:

* Reply with a friendly greeting
* Ask for product details
* Ask for quantity
* Ask for delivery city
* Ask for company information
* Ask whether they are buying for own use or resale
* Send selected catalog pages
* Send selected product photos
* Send item-based quotation
* Do not send full price list yet
* Move to nurture list
* Stop following

### 8. Suggested WhatsApp Reply

Write a short, professional WhatsApp reply.

The reply should be:

* Polite
* Clear
* Direct
* Not too long
* Suitable for B2B sales
* Not too aggressive
* Focused on getting the missing information needed for a proper quotation

Please provide 2 versions:

Version A: Friendly and professional

Version B: More direct and sales-focused

### 9. Internal Sales Notes

Write short internal notes for the salesperson:

* Lead quality:
* Buyer type:
* Missing information:
* Main risk:
* Recommended action:

### 10. Final Recommendation

Choose one:

* Quote now
* Ask more questions first
* Send catalog only
* Send selected product photos
* Send limited information
* Nurture
* Do not continue

Explain why.

## Important Rules

Do not make unsupported claims.

If information is missing, say “unknown” instead of guessing.

Do not assume someone is a competitor without evidence.

Do not use pushy or spammy wording.

Do not recommend sending a full price list unless the lead is qualified.

Focus on practical B2B sales decisions.
