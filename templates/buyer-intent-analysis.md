## 4. Buyer Intent Signals

### Strong Intent Signals

* Specific product requirement
* Quantity mentioned
* Delivery city or country mentioned
* Project, hotel, store, warehouse, or company name provided
* Clear buying timeline
* Company information provided
* Asks about availability, MOQ, delivery, customization, documents, samples, or payment terms
* Provides photos, specifications, or reference products
* Responds clearly to follow-up questions

### Weak Intent Signals

* Only says "price?"
* No company name
* No quantity
* No delivery city
* No product details
* Refuses to answer basic questions
* Asks for a full price list immediately
* Asks many unrelated product prices without clear demand
* Gives vague answers after repeated follow-up

## 8. Suggested Follow-up Message

Example message:

Hi [Name], thank you for your inquiry.

To prepare a suitable quotation, may I know:

1. Which items do you need?
2. How many cartons / pieces do you need?
3. Which city should we deliver to?
4. Are you buying for your own project, hotel, store, or for resale?

Once we have these details, we can check availability and prepare the most suitable offer for you.
