# Lead Quality Scorecard Template

Use this template to score and classify a B2B lead before sending a quotation, catalog, full price list, or follow-up message.

This scorecard is designed for B2B exporters, manufacturers, and international sales teams.

## 3. Score Explanation

### Company Information Provided - 15 Points

Give full points if the lead provides:

- Company name
- Website or social media page
- Business location
- Company role or business type

Give partial or zero points if the customer only uses a personal profile or does not share company details.

### Clear Product Requirement - 20 Points

Give full points if the customer clearly mentions specific products.

Examples:

- Hotel shampoo
- Shower gel
- Soap
- Hotel slippers
- Towels
- Housekeeping items
- Cold room panels
- Commercial equipment

Give partial points if the requirement is general but still relevant.

### Quantity Mentioned - 20 Points

Give full points if the customer mentions:

- Carton quantity
- Piece quantity
- Project quantity
- Room count
- Monthly demand
- Trial order quantity

Quantity is important because quotation accuracy depends on volume.

### Delivery City or Country Mentioned - 10 Points

Give full points if the customer mentions:

- Delivery city
- Country
- Project location
- Branch location
- Warehouse location

Delivery location affects availability, logistics, and final quotation.

### Contact Role Relevance - 15 Points

Give full points if the contact appears to be:

- Owner
- General manager
- Procurement manager
- Purchasing manager
- Operations manager
- Project manager
- Distributor buyer
- Contractor
- Decision maker or influencer

Give lower points if the role is unclear or unrelated.

### Urgency or Timeline Mentioned - 10 Points

Give full points if the customer mentions:

- Immediate need
- Delivery deadline
- Project timeline
- Opening date
- Tender deadline
- Reorder timing

### Communication Quality - 10 Points

Give full points if the customer communicates clearly, answers questions, and provides useful details.

Give lower points if the message is vague, inconsistent, or only asks for price without context.

## 4. Lead Classification

| Score | Classification | Meaning |
|---:|---|---|
| 80-100 | Hot lead | Strong buying intent. Prepare quotation or follow up quickly. |
| 60-79 | Qualified lead | Good potential. Ask for missing details before quotation. |
| 40-59 | Nurture lead | Some interest, but needs more qualification. |
| 20-39 | Low-priority lead | Limited information. Do not send detailed pricing yet. |
| 0-19 | Unqualified lead | Not enough information or not relevant. |

## 5. Recommended Action by Score

### 80-100: Hot Lead

Recommended action:

- Prepare item-based quotation
- Confirm stock and delivery time
- Move quickly to WhatsApp, email, or sales call
- Send relevant product photos or catalog pages
- Follow up within a short time

### 60-79: Qualified Lead

Recommended action:

- Ask for missing information
- Confirm quantity and delivery city
- Send selected catalog pages
- Prepare quotation after details are clear

### 40-59: Nurture Lead

Recommended action:

- Ask qualification questions
- Send company introduction
- Share selected product categories
- Do not send full price list yet
- Add to nurture list

### 20-39: Low-Priority Lead

Recommended action:

- Ask for company name
- Ask for quantity
- Ask for delivery city
- Ask whether buying for own use or resale
- Share limited product information only

### 0-19: Unqualified Lead

Recommended action:

- Do not send full quotation
- Do not send full price list
- Ask for basic information first
- Stop following if the lead remains vague or irrelevant
