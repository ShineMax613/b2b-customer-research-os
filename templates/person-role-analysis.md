## 6. Buying Intent Signals

Strong signals:

- Mentions specific product needs
- Shares company or project details
- Mentions quantity
- Mentions delivery city
- Asks about availability, MOQ, customization, samples, documents, or delivery time
- Responds clearly to follow-up questions
- Uses business language related to procurement or operations

Weak signals:

- Only asks for price
- No company information
- No clear role
- No quantity or product details
- Avoids basic questions
- Asks for full price list immediately
- Profile does not match the claimed business
- Communication looks unrelated to purchasing
