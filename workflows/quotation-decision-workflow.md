# Quotation Decision Workflow

Use this workflow to decide whether to send a quotation, catalog, selected product photos, limited information, or ask more questions before sharing price information.

This workflow is designed for B2B exporters, manufacturers, wholesalers, and international sales teams.

## 1. Purpose

Many B2B export sales teams send quotations too early.

A customer may ask:

- Price?
- Send catalog
- Send full price list
- Best price?
- Wholesale price?

But before sending pricing, the salesperson should understand:

- Who the buyer is
- What products they need
- What quantity they need
- Where the goods should be delivered
- Whether they are buying for own use or resale
- Whether they are an end user, distributor, contractor, trader, or competitor
- Whether the lead is worth a detailed quotation

This workflow helps sales teams make a better quotation decision.

## 2. Step 1 - Collect Basic Lead Information

Before deciding what to send, collect the following information:

| Field | Status |
|---|---|
| Company name | Provided / Missing |
| Contact person name | Provided / Missing |
| Buyer role | Provided / Missing |
| Product requirement | Clear / Partially clear / Missing |
| Quantity | Provided / Missing |
| Delivery city or country | Provided / Missing |
| Buying purpose | Own use / Resale / Project / Unknown |
| Timeline or urgency | Provided / Missing |
| Custom branding requirement | Yes / No / Unknown |

## 3. Step 2 - Identify Buyer Type

Classify the lead as one or more of the following:

- End user
- Distributor
- Wholesaler
- Contractor
- Project buyer
- Trader
- Competitor
- Unknown

## 4. Step 3 - Check Product Requirement Clarity

### Clear Requirement

A requirement is clear when the customer provides:

- Specific product name
- Size, model, material, or specification
- Quantity
- Delivery location
- Usage scenario or buying purpose

Recommended action:

- Prepare item-based quotation
- Send relevant product photos
- Confirm stock and delivery time

### Partially Clear Requirement

A requirement is partially clear when the customer provides:

- Product category but no exact item
- Product interest but no quantity
- Quantity but no delivery city
- Delivery city but no exact product

Recommended action:

- Ask missing questions first
- Send selected catalog pages if helpful
- Avoid sending full price list

### Unclear Requirement

A requirement is unclear when the customer only says:

- Price?
- Catalog?
- Send all prices
- Best price
- Need hotel supplies
- Need products

Recommended action:

- Ask qualification questions first
- Send company introduction if needed
- Do not send full quotation yet

## 5. Step 4 - Check Lead Quality

Use a simple lead quality level:

### High-Quality Lead

Signals:

- Company name provided
- Specific product requirement
- Quantity mentioned
- Delivery city mentioned
- Relevant buyer role
- Clear buying purpose
- Professional communication

Recommended action:

- Send item-based quotation
- Follow up quickly
- Move to WhatsApp, email, or sales call if appropriate

### Medium-Quality Lead

Signals:

- Relevant product inquiry
- Some information provided
- Quantity or delivery city missing
- Buyer role unclear
- Still possible real demand

Recommended action:

- Ask for missing details
- Send limited product information
- Prepare quotation after clarification

### Low-Quality Lead

Signals:

- No company name
- No quantity
- No delivery city
- No clear product requirement
- Asks only for price
- Avoids basic qualification questions

Recommended action:

- Ask basic questions first
- Share limited information only
- Do not send full price list

## 6. Step 5 - Decide What to Send

### Send Full Quotation

Send a quotation when:

- Product requirement is clear
- Quantity is confirmed
- Delivery city is confirmed
- Buyer type is reasonably understood
- Lead quality is medium or high
- No major red flags exist

### Send Item-Based Quotation

Send item-based quotation when:

- Customer asks for specific items
- Quantity is provided
- Stock availability can be checked
- Delivery location is known

This is usually safer than sending a full price list.

### Send Selected Catalog Pages

Send selected catalog pages when:

- Product category is clear
- Customer needs options
- Quantity is not confirmed yet
- Customer is still exploring

Avoid sending the full catalog with sensitive information if the lead is not qualified.

### Send Selected Product Photos

Send selected photos when:

- Customer asks what is available
- Customer needs visual confirmation
- You want to keep the conversation active
- You are not ready to quote yet

### Ask More Questions First

Ask more questions when:

- Quantity is missing
- Delivery city is missing
- Company name is missing
- Buyer role is unclear
- Buying purpose is unknown
- Customer asks for full price list immediately

### Do Not Send Full Price List Yet

Avoid sending full price list when:

- Customer is not qualified
- Customer asks for many unrelated SKUs
- Customer gives no quantity
- Customer gives no company information
- Customer may be a competitor or price hunter
- Customer refuses to answer basic questions

## 7. Qualification Questions

Use these questions before sending quotation:

1. May I know your company name?
2. Which items are you interested in?
3. How many cartons or pieces do you need?
4. Which city or country should we deliver to?
5. Are you buying for your own hotel, project, store, or for resale?
6. Do you need regular stock items or customized branding?
7. When do you need the goods?

## 8. Suggested WhatsApp Reply Before Quotation

Hi [Name], thank you for your inquiry.

To prepare an accurate quotation, may I know which items you need, the quantity, and the delivery city?

Also, are you buying for your own project / hotel / store, or for resale?

Once we have these details, we can check availability and prepare the most suitable offer for you.

## 9. Suggested Reply When Customer Asks for Full Price List

Hi [Name], thank you for your interest.

Because prices depend on item, quantity, packaging, stock availability, and delivery location, we usually prepare quotations based on confirmed requirements instead of sending a general full price list.

Please share the items you need, quantity, and delivery city, and we will prepare a suitable offer for you.

## 10. Decision Table

| Situation | Recommended Action |
|---|---|
| Specific item + quantity + delivery city | Send item-based quotation |
| Specific product category but no quantity | Ask quantity first |
| Quantity provided but no delivery city | Ask delivery city |
| Customer asks for full price list immediately | Ask qualification questions first |
| Customer is likely distributor | Ask about regular demand and fast-moving items |
| Customer is likely end user | Focus on stock, delivery, quality, and service |
| Customer is likely contractor | Ask for BOQ, specs, project details, and documents |
| Customer may be competitor | Share limited information only |
| Customer remains vague | Do not send full pricing |

## 11. Final Decision

Choose one:

- Send item-based quotation
- Send selected catalog pages
- Send selected product photos
- Ask more questions first
- Send company introduction only
- Send limited information only
- Add to nurture list
- Do not continue
